How we work

A Florida academy, honest with parents

Miha Gregorc Tennis Academy LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L22000001475, filed in 2022, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

Coaches working with players under 18 are background screened and a parent or guardian must complete registration, medical and consent forms before a minor takes part. Florida heat is a genuine hazard, so sessions follow a written hydration and break protocol and play is suspended in extreme conditions. We are not medical providers — a player with an injury or condition needs clearance from their physician. And we promise no rankings, results or scholarships, because nobody honestly can.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will my child get a college scholarship?

We will never promise that, and be wary of any academy that does. We will give you an honest assessment of where your child stands and what the realistic path looks like, which is more useful.

How do you decide which group?

By a free on-court assessment, and by standard rather than age. We also move players between groups when the fit changes, and we will tell you why.

What about the heat?

Taken seriously. There is a written hydration and break protocol, and play is suspended in extreme conditions. Any player showing signs of heat illness is removed and not returned the same day.

Are coaches background checked?

Yes, for anyone working with minors, and a parent or guardian completes consent and medical forms before a child participates. Ask any academy this question.

I am an adult beginner. Will I be out of my depth?

No. Beginner sessions are genuinely for beginners, and adults starting later are our most common new players.

My child has an injury. Can they train?

Not without clearance from their physician. We are coaches, not medical providers, and we will not work around an injury we are not qualified to assess.

Not sure which group fits?

A free on-court assessment, and an honest view of the right level.
