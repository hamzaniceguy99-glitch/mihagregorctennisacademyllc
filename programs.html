Our programmes, in detail

Four programmes for juniors and adults, all grouped by playing standard.

Program 01 · The right group matters most

Junior development squads

Grouping by age is convenient for timetables and bad for players. We assess and group by standard, so a session is challenging without being demoralising — and we will move a player up or down when the group stops fitting them, which is a conversation we have with the parent honestly.

  • Weekly
  • Juniors under 18
  • Per term or block
  • Consent form

Program 02 · One thing at a time

Private & semi-private lessons

Private lessons work when they have a single focus — a serve that breaks down under pressure, a backhand that cannot be hit on the run. Trying to fix everything in an hour fixes nothing, so we pick one thing and work on it until it holds in a match.

  • Per lesson
  • All ages and standards
  • Per lesson or block
  • Court booking

Program 03 · Beginners genuinely welcome

Adult programmes

Adults starting at forty are our most common new players and the most likely to be put off by a group that is beyond them. Beginner sessions are genuinely for beginners, and we would rather you enjoyed a session at the right level than survived one at the wrong one.

  • Weekly
  • Adults
  • Per term or block
  • Nothing to prepare

Program 04 · Honest about the odds

Match play & tournament prep

Players improve through matches more than through drills, so match play is supervised and reviewed. On the bigger questions — college tennis, rankings, going further — we give parents an honest assessment rather than the one that sells another programme. Nobody here promises a scholarship.
